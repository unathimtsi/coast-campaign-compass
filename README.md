# Coast Campaign Compass

Build me a very basic website for a campaign called Cruising The Coastline at Thirsti (the water company), this campaign spans the summer and we essentially give out complimentary waters to the public in different locations along the KZN coast. I want to build a website that serves the primary purpose of communicating to the team what events are happening at what places along the campaign route and thus help us optimise our route to capitalise on those, it must feature a calendar and that calendar will tell them which area the campaign head has decided we need to activate that day and what is happening there that day, it also has secondary options based on the forecast, so for instance if it's raining there that day it'll say that based on the weather forecast for the day the advised place to activate that day is another beach maybe 50km down the coast from there etc. so it'll feature a map as well that shows different events based on the different days. (base your calender and map and such on the pdf provided)  Keep it simple but effective please, this is an internal site not for the public, just for our activations/promotions team and the marketing manager to communicate this info effectively.
